## Full description (max 4000)
Norteia is a no-nonsense compass with GPS: open it and everything is already on
screen. No menus, no login, no ads.

◆ ACCURATE COMPASS
• Fused orientation sensor (gyroscope + accelerometer + magnetometer) for a
  steady needle that doesn't jitter.
• Automatic magnetic declination: switch between True North and Magnetic North
  with one tap.
• Clear prompt when it's time to calibrate (the figure-8) or when the phone is
  tilted too far.
• A carefully drawn compass rose, smooth animation, and a light haptic tick as
  you cross each cardinal point.

◆ FULL GPS
• Latitude and longitude with horizontal accuracy shown in metres.
• Altitude, speed and course over ground.
• Live satellites: how many are used, how many in view, and signal strength.
• Warning when a mock location is active on the device.

◆ COORDINATES YOUR WAY
• Decimal degrees, degrees/minutes/seconds, UTM and MGRS.
• Tap to change format; copy, share, or open the point in any maps app.

◆ USEFUL EXTRAS
• Sun and Moon position (azimuth, elevation, rise/set, Moon phase) — all
  computed on the device.
• Built-in bubble level.

◆ BUILT TO RESPECT YOU
• Works offline.
• No internet permission.
• No background location.
• No ads in this version. No analytics. No trackers.
• Your location data never leaves the device and is never stored.

Great for hiking, camping, travel, fishing, aligning a satellite dish, geography
class, or just getting your bearings in a new city.

Notes:
• The compass needs the device magnetometer; some tablets don't have one.
• Keep away from magnets, metal and magnetic-clasp cases for better readings.
